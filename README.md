# Carolina Cerón — Digital Portfolio

> Design, direction & web development for digital strategy and brand positioning.

---

### About the Project

Designed and built as a bespoke web editorial space for **Carolina Cerón Escobar**, a Viña del Mar-based digital strategist and brand consultant with over five years of experience in visual communication and performance marketing.

The layout blends a modern, tactile aesthetic—inspired by postage stamps and fine typography—with a curated pastel palette and high-contrast dark plum accents.
